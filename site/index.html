Everything you need for a quick walkthrough

Record anything

The whole screen, one window, every window of an app, or an area you drag out.

You, in a bubble

Add your camera as a circle or rounded square, drag it anywhere, resize it, mirror it.

Voice and system sound

Record your microphone and your Mac's audio, from every app or just one.

Pause, then carry on

Pause and resume as often as you like. If Visor crashes, the recording is saved up to the last half second.

Shortcuts from any app

Start, stop, pause, mark or discard a recording from whatever app you're in.

Mark each step

Drop a marker as you go and name it later. Exports get a chapter at each one.

Trim, cut, speed up

Cut the stumbles, trim the ends, drag a cut's edge to the exact frame, and play it at 0.5× to 2×.

Cut the pauses

One click cuts every pause of 2 seconds or more where nothing moves and nothing is said.

Blur what's private

Hide a password, an email address or a notification, for the whole video or just a moment.

Export your way

MP4, WebM or GIF at 1080p, 720p or full size, with background noise reduced and the volume evened out.

Stays out of the shot

Visor's own controls and camera bubble never show up in what you record.

Nothing leaves your Mac

Recordings are plain folders on your disk. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

From idea to shareable video in a minute

  1. Pick what to record from the menu bar: a screen, a window, an app or an area, plus your camera and mic.
  2. Hit record. A short countdown, then a small control bar to pause, stop or start over.
  3. Name it, trim it, export it. The editor opens right away, ready for a name. Cut, speed up, export, and paste the file wherever it needs to go.

Or let an AI agent record the demo

Ask Claude Code, Cursor or another AI agent to show how something works, and it can make the video itself.

  • It records and marks each step, and pauses while it sets something up or tries again.
  • It checks what it recorded, frame by frame, and cuts what went wrong or where nothing happens.
  • It hands the video to you in the editor, where you can undo any of its cuts.

It's off until you turn on Let AI agents control recording in Preferences. Then add Visor to your agent. With Claude Code: 

claude mcp add visor -- /Applications/Visor.app/Contents/MacOS/visor mcp

Cursor, Claude Desktop and other agents take the same command where they add tools (MCP servers). Preferences shows it too.

Questions

What permissions does Visor need?

Screen Recording, which macOS asks for the first time you record (you may need to reopen Visor after allowing it), and the camera and microphone when you choose them. Visor is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any app you download.

Where do my recordings go?

To ~/Movies/Visor, one folder per recording, which you can change in Preferences. Edits never touch the original tracks, so you can always go back and export again.

Does Visor connect to the internet?

Only to check for updates, and only if you say yes when it asks. The check downloads a small list of versions from this site; it sends no account, no recordings and nothing about what you do. Every update is signed, and Visor refuses one that doesn't match its signature. You can also check by hand from the menu bar: Check for Updates…

Can an agent record without me knowing?

No. Agents can't control Visor until you turn on Let AI agents control recording in Preferences, and a recording an agent starts says Agent on the control bar. Your own controls and shortcuts keep working, so you can pause, stop or discard it at any time. Commands only reach Visor from your own user account on your Mac.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

From any app: ⌥⇧R starts a recording with your last choices, or stops one; ⌥⇧P pauses or resumes; ⌥⇧M adds a marker; ⌥⇧⌫ discards. You can turn them off in Preferences, which also tells you if another app already uses one.

Does it work on Windows, Linux or Intel Macs?

Not yet. Visor runs on Apple Silicon Macs today. Windows and Linux versions are being built and need testers; follow the project on GitHub.

Is it really free?

Yes. Visor is open source under the GPL-3.0 licence. There's no account, no trial and nothing to upgrade to. Donations are optional.
